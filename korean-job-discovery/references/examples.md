# Examples (workflow examples, not verified vacancies)

- “직무는 아직 안 정했어. 대전에서 일할 곳을 넓게 찾아줘.”
  General/any first. Clarify duties when useful; don't assume engineering,
  university graduation, an age group or entry level. Find local sources in Daejeon.
- “서울 경력 3년 총무·인사와 영업지원 채용을 찾아줘.”
  Office/sales, broad boards plus matching career-level sources and official JDs.
  Don't force accounting-office jobs or startup rosters.
- “부산 편집디자인·영상편집 일자리를 알아봐. 프리랜서는 제외.”
  Design/media; Designerjob/Mediajob plus regional sources. Distinguish employment
  from project work and verify portfolio requirements and contract type.
- “수원 학원 영어강사나 교육운영 공고를 찾아줘.”
  Education + Hunjang, general boards and relevant institution notices. A school
  teacher license isn't a universal academy-instructor requirement. Gyeonggi
  education-office openings may need different credentials; check separately.
- “대구 병원 원무·행정과 사회복지시설 사무직을 비교해줘.”
  Healthcare/welfare/office, duties-first. Don't assume clinical licensure or infer
  eligibility from the platform's nursing/social-work focus. Verify each role.
- “인천 주말 카페·판매 알바를 찾아줘. 오전 근무만.”
  Part_time/hospitality/retail, Albamon/Alba/Daangn/Shopma as relevant. Record actual
  hours, pay basis, workplace and direct employer. Platform-owned employer notice
  can be issuer_platform with ownership evidence; a repost alone cannot.
- “창원 생산·포장·창고 일자리, 교대 없는 곳을 알아봐.”
  Manufacturing/logistics; Work24/Incruit occupation filters plus local sources.
  Don't substitute construction or engineering jobs; preserve shift restrictions.
- “중장년 재취업 지원과 사무직 채용을 함께 알려줘.”
  Office plus explicitly requested midcareer support. Separate counseling/training
  from live jobs, and verify program eligibility without collecting unnecessary data.
- “원티드·사람인 말고 판매직을 찾을 플랫폼만 조사해줘.”
  Channels mode, retail, explicit source exclusions; no vacancy recommendations.
- “A회사 채용만 알아봐.”
  Company mode; official careers and employer-name/duty searches; no unrelated rosters.
- “부산 신입 QA·기술지원 공고를 찾아줘.”
  IT/junior and relevant region. Technical sources remain available when requested.
- “Read skills/korean-job-discovery/SKILL.md. Research Seoul marketing and sales
  jobs with unrestricted experience levels. Return run JSON and a Korean coverage
  report. Use available web tools; don't apply, contact or subscribe.”
  Read routing/catalog and verification/handoff. Helpers generate plans, not searches.
